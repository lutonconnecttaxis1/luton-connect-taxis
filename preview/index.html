Who we are

Luton Connect Taxis is a local taxi operator covering everything from everyday local trips and school runs to corporate accounts, national travel and airport transfers. Whether you’re heading across the country or flying abroad, we watch your flight, wait where you land and take you door to door – with a clear estimate up front and a real person on the end of the phone.
